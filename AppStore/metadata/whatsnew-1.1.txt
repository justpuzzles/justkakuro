A new Kakuro every day. The Daily card on Home has the same puzzle for everyone: small boards early in the week, medium ones midweek, and a large board every Saturday and Sunday, free whether or not you have unlocked the full game. Solve it on consecutive days to build a streak; Stats shows your current and best streak for free.

The board can now be played with VoiceOver. Every cell names its runs, hints are read aloud, and text, contrast and touch targets follow your accessibility settings.

A swipe from the left edge no longer drops you out of a live puzzle, and the timer pauses the moment you leave a board.
